## Current firmware behavior

usb-host-s3/src/main.cpp generates a continuous low-volume square wave at
8 kHz sample rate, starting at 440 Hz. USB MIDI Note On changes its pitch
and toggles the onboard RGB LED in blue. Note Off is not handled, so the
tone intentionally continues after releasing a key.
USB CDC on boot is disabled in platformio.ini; the intended arrangement
is laptop serial through USB-to-UART and keyboard through native USB/OTG.
The physical port identities still need confirmation from board labels
or a photo; left/right descriptions are not reliable.

## Reset diagnosis: boot strap sampled low

User observed no green LED after diagnostic upload, then green after pressing
RESET. A serial read after RESET received steady diagnostic heartbeats:
uptime 43199..48199 ms, reset=POWERON (1), GPIO0=1, heartbeat 24..29.
The pulse appeared steady to the user; timing/serial backpressure may affect
visual cadence, so LED cadence alone is not a crash diagnosis.

User then unplugged/replugged without pressing buttons: no green LED.

A ROM connection with mode=no-reset and two register reads returned:
- GPIO_STRAP_REG (0x60004038): 0x0
- GPIO0 SPI-boot strap bit (1 << 3): clear, GPIO0 sampled LOW
- RTC_CNTL_OPTION1_REG (0x6000812C): 0x0
- FORCE_DOWNLOAD_BOOT bit (0x1): clear

Thus the captured bootloader session has a low GPIO0 boot strap and no
software force-download flag. GPIO0 was high when the app ran after manual
RESET. Suspect power-up GPIO0/BOOT/reset behavior or host-side USB effects;
the measurements do not prove a permanent GPIO0 short or a damaged board.

Pending isolation test: bare board powered only from a USB wall charger or
power bank on the same connector, no laptop USB/data, no button presses.
Observe whether green starts automatically. This distinguishes a possible
laptop/USB-related trigger from power-up strap behavior independent of it.
